# Checksums

SHA-256 of the jars in the [Steam Workshop item](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3810710740),
per version. How to rebuild and compare them yourself:
[Verifying the Workshop jars](README.md#verifying-the-workshop-jars).
